# DemonDive Alpha 7.4D — Checkpoint 02 Handoff

## Roadmap position

Active phase: **Alpha 7.4D — deterministic campaign/Deep Dive integrity and balance evidence**.

This checkpoint expands automated integrity evidence. It does not substitute for fresh/max-save campaign playthroughs or the required human two-hour Deep Dive.

## Automated campaign contracts

- Level 1 remains 7–8 rooms; each campaign level adds two rooms, reaching 23–24 in Level 9.
- Every sampled plan retains the midpoint mini-boss, final boss and no-consecutive-empty-room rule.
- Health, damage and cadence increase within every level and across campaign stages.
- Elite room pressure is higher than an ordinary combat room at the same position.
- The campaign threat function has no permanent-upgrade input, protecting the rule against hidden scaling to the player's save.

## Automated Deep Dive contracts

- A 300-room schedule fixture verifies ten-room biome blocks, mini-boss slot 6 and boss slot 10.
- Adjacent blocks change biome and the route uses left, right, top and bottom.
- Minimum enemy pressure, threat, cross-pollination and stacked boss powers do not regress with depth.
- Double bosses begin at cycle 3; reward, wager and route-choice cadence remain encounter-compatible.
- Final Deep Dive room metadata is now rebuilt after altered-arena enemies and hazards are inserted.

## Smart boon curation evidence

- A deterministic 600-run cohort samples ten offers per run using the two-choice rule.
- Starting-boon investment remains favored into the intended middle without becoming guaranteed.
- The cohort must retain both low starter-offer runs and rare eight-or-more starter-offer runs.
- New-boon discovery must remain stronger than starter repetition across the whole cohort.

## Still required before Alpha 7.4D exit

- Human fresh-save and max-upgrade Levels 1–9 completion.
- Seeded challenge-room and boss difficulty ratings.
- Obscene-build logical/readability approval and a two-hour Deep Dive trace.
- Physical keyboard/controller and representative desktop/laptop performance captures.
- P0/P1 defect closure and explicit P2/P3 disposition.
