# DemonDive Alpha 7.7B — Checkpoint 01

The missing Level 4–9 narrative structure is now locked as 24 unique trigger IDs: entrance, mini-boss introduction, boss introduction, and aftermath for each level. Canon constraints are attached to every beat so later dialogue cannot quietly turn Demon Lords into ordinary humans, turn mechanics into undisclosed surprises, or contradict the acceptance ending.

The ending order is also contract-tested: Milo wakes, apologizes, puts the weed away, cleans one corner, sees the queens on television, gets pizza, and accepts rather than destroys The Hollow.

Final dialogue lines are not yet approved and are therefore not wired into runtime progression. This prevents placeholder prose from blocking campaign transitions or being mistaken for final canon.
